# m.ledger

**Record with rigor. Check it against your statement. See your net worth clearly.**

m.ledger is a personal accounting app for Android, built on double-entry bookkeeping. Organize your money into books, accounts, and entries, import bank statements, check each movement, and follow your net worth, income, and expenses on a dashboard, all stored securely on your device. It runs on Android 8.0 or later and is available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.

## What m.ledger does

- Organize your finances into independent books by currency and hierarchical accounts (Assets, Liabilities, Income, Expenses, Equity), with optional suggested accounts.
- Record entries with origin and destination postings that always balance to zero. Correct entries without erasing using reversals and edits with audit trails.
- Reconcile movements against bank statements and import CSV or OFX statement files with built-in duplicate detection.
- Follow your net worth, assets, liabilities, income, and expenses on a dashboard featuring monthly cash flow, category breakdowns, and net worth evolution charts.
- Receive local alerts for learned recurrences and spending level changes, calculated entirely on your device.
- Export CSV reference files, create ZIP backups, and restore your ledger. Optionally connect Google Drive for automated backups.

You can record entries, import local files, browse dashboards, and view statements without an internet connection or a Google account. Daily update checks, automatic Google Drive backups, and recovery require internet access.

## Get started

Download the current Android APK from the [m.ledger releases](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/mledger-releases/releases) page. Install the app on your device running Android 8.0 or later, explore the starter book, and record or import your first entries. The [project README](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/mledger-releases#readme) has more details about the app and its features.

## Your data

Your m.ledger accounting data is stored on your device. You control whether to connect Google Drive, upload a backup, or export CSV files. Read the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details about these choices and data handling.
