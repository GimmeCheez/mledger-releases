# m.ledger Terms of Use

**Effective date: October 28, 2026**

These terms explain how you may use m.ledger, an Android app for personal accounting based on double-entry bookkeeping. By using the app, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

## Using m.ledger

m.ledger supports independent books by currency, hierarchical accounts across five types, double-entry postings, bank statement reconciliation, and CSV/OFX statement imports. You are responsible for the content you record, import, export, or share and for using the app in accordance with applicable rules and financial practices. m.ledger does not provide financial, tax, or legal advice.

## Optional Google services and updates

Google Drive automatic backup is optional. You choose whether to authorize access, turn on automatic backups, and upload backups to your private app folder. You can disconnect the account or revoke access through your Google Account settings.

Daily update checks query GitHub to notify you of new app versions. This check is optional and public.

## Your data and backups

Your accounting data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for keeping a backup if you want to preserve your records when switching devices, clearing app data, or uninstalling. Restoring a backup replaces all current data in the app; export or back it up first if you need to retain it. Google Drive backup is an optional additional method. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details about data handling.

## App results and availability

This is an early version. Test the app on your own device before trusting it with real financial data. Calculations, summaries, charts, local alerts, and account suggestions are aids to managing your finances, not definitive classifications. Review any result or report that matters to you. Features may depend on device resources, file formats, an internet connection, or the availability of Google services. The app may change as it develops.

These terms may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version; the updated terms will be published here. For questions or to report an issue, use the [m.ledger releases repository's Issues tab](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/mledger-releases/issues).
